Creaking Hinge

Horror for iPhone and iPad

A horror game about opening a door very, very slowly. On iPhone Duo, the real hinge opens the door. On every other iPhone and iPad, an on-screen slider does. Ease it open, take the relic, and close it again without a sound. Too fast, and the hinge creaks. Too many creaks, and it hears you.

  • 100 doors
  • Christmas mode
  • iPhone and iPad
  • Real hinge on iPhone Duo
Download on the App Store Help Privacy

See it played

How it works

  1. Hold it like a book. Fold your iPhone Duo back to about 85°, where the door reads 0%, and hold still for a moment to begin.
  2. Unfold slowly. The door opens with your phone. Watch the speed meter: open too fast and the hinge creaks.
  3. Tap the relic. Each level hides relics behind the door. Tap one when it comes into view to take it.
  4. Close it without a sound. Ease the door shut again. Keep the "It hears you" meter below 100%, or it finds you.

No iPhone Duo? Every other iPhone and iPad gets an on-screen slider: drag it slowly right to open and left to close. Same 100 doors, same purchases.
