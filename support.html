Support

Help for Glide and Creaking Hinge. Email support@luxwebventures.com and we'll usually reply within two working days. Please tell us which game, your iPhone or iPad model, and your iOS version.

Both gamesGlideCreaking Hinge

For both games

Do I need an iPhone Duo? No. Both games play on any iPhone, with the same levels and purchases. On other iPhones, Glide uses touch and Creaking Hinge uses an on-screen slider.

I bought something on another device, or reinstalled. Purchases restore on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Glide: Settings > Restore purchases & progress. Creaking Hinge: Shop > Restore purchases.

I want a refund. Purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy. The games collect no personal information themselves. Ads come from Google AdMob. You can change your ad choices with the Privacy choices button in Settings (Ad privacy choices in Creaking Hinge), and stop tracking in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Glide

How to play

Throw from the launch hill by flicking right and slightly up. A quick, slightly upward flick is a Perfect throw. Steer with the hinge on iPhone Duo (open to climb, close to dive), with tilt, or by dragging up and down on any iPhone. Climbing costs speed and diving builds it. Air rises under clouds. Pop bubbles, avoid birds, and grab green boosters to reach the flag.

iPhone Duo and other iPhones

  • iPhone Duo, open: play in landscape and choose Hinge or Tilt on the front page. Steering a whole flight with the hinge earns a Hinge bonus on coins.
  • iPhone Duo, folded: play upright on the cover screen and steer with tilt.
  • Other iPhones: play in landscape and steer by touch (drag up and down). Settings > Touch controls turns on touch on any iPhone.

Common questions

I can't find hinge or tilt controls. Those are for iPhone Duo only.

The plane climbs or dives when my phone is level. Tap Reset on the ready screen, or use Settings > Hinge setup (or Calibrate tilt).

I closed my iPhone Duo mid-flight. The game pauses. Unfold to carry on with the hinge, or tap Keep flying with tilt.

I'd rather not fold or tilt. Turn on Settings > Touch controls.

Moving my progress to a new iPhone. Progress syncs through your own iCloud account. If it hasn't appeared, use Settings > Restore purchases & progress, and check that iCloud is turned on for Glide.

My scores aren't on the leaderboards. Sign in to Game Center (iPhone Settings > Game Center), then reopen Glide.

I don't want ads between levels. Buy Remove Ads or All Worlds in the Shop.

The "Watch an ad" button is missing. It only appears when an ad is ready. Check your connection and try again.

Creaking Hinge

How to play

Hold your iPhone open like a book. The door is shut at 85° on the hinge: fold back to about 85° (the door reads 0%) and hold it there for a moment to begin. Then unfold slowly and the door opens with your phone. Tap a relic when it comes into view, then close the door again without a sound. Too fast, and the hinge creaks. Too many creaks, and it hears you.

iPhone Duo, other iPhones and iPad

  • iPhone Duo: play on the inside screen, and the real hinge opens the door. Folding shut pauses the game until you unfold.
  • Other iPhones and iPad: a slider at the bottom of the screen opens the door. Drag it slowly right to open, and left to close.

Common questions

The door stopped following my phone. Hold still and tap Reset hinge (the circular arrow at the top of the screen).

I'd rather not fold the phone. Go to Settings > Hinge and turn on "Use on-screen slider".

It's too scary. Settings > Comfort has Reduce jumpscare intensity and Reduce flashing.

I don't want ads. Buy Remove Ads in the Shop. It also makes second chances free.

Moving my progress to a new iPhone. Purchases restore with your Apple Account, but progress (stars, coins, unlocked levels) is saved on the device only.
